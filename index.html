# MARGERAS Privacy Policy **Last updated: October 6, 2026** MARGERAS is a community bot for Root communities. ## Information We Use MARGERAS may access information provided by the Root platform that is necessary to perform its functions, such as a member's Root user ID and community membership information. ## How We Use Information MARGERAS uses this information only to automatically assign the existing **Member** role to new members of the community. ## Data Storage MARGERAS does not intentionally store, sell, or share personal information with third parties. ## Third-Party Services MARGERAS operates through the Root platform and may receive information made available to the bot through Root's APIs and services. ## Data Retention MARGERAS does not maintain a separate database of member personal information. ## Contact For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or MARGERAS, please contact the bot owner through the Root community where MARGERAS is installed. ## Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the functionality of MARGERAS changes. The latest version will always be available at this page.
